# Changelog

All notable changes to cortex-vendrobbery are listed here. Versions follow semantic versioning.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-24)

First release.

### Added
- Break into any Cortex Vending soda, snack or coffee machine by hand, on the machine itself.
- Two ways through the door: pick its seven-pin lock with a tension wrench and a pick, or lever it off three latches with a crowbar. Picking is quiet, the crowbar always rings the alarm.
- Every lock is pinned at random. One pin binds at a time, and the server alone knows the order and the depths.
- Inside: pull the bill stacker off the door, pick or drill the cash box and take the bundles, and pull stock off the shelves.
- A picked door gives you notes that open the cash box faster. A crowbar thief picks it blind or drills it.
- The take is real. A player's machine pays out of its owner's cash box, a Vendex machine pays out of what it sold, and stolen stock leaves the shelves.
- Other players see the door standing open, and the machine is out of order until it repairs itself or its owner pays for a new lock.
- Strikes for mistakes: a jammed door lock, a seized cash box, a snapped drill bit, and an alarm with a police alert.
- Owners are told when their machine's alarm goes off and when it has been robbed.
- `/vendrob` admin command, a Discord audit log through a server convar, and a work light toggle in the Cortex settings tab.
